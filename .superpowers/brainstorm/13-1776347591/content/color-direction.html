What should the app's primary color identity be?

Right now the app has 3 different color systems fighting each other. Pick the direction we lock everything to.

Deep Blue — Keep & Unify

Lock the whole app to the existing blue you already use in Login/Register/Home. Workout screen gets refactored to match. Clean, professional, medical-adjacent — works well for fitness tracking.

Indigo / Deep Purple — Upgrade

Shift from sky-blue to a richer indigo/deep-purple. More premium feel, still cohesive with fitness. Login, workout, everything moves to this single seed color.

Teal / Green — Lean into Workout Screen

The workout screen already uses teal. Flip the whole app to match it — teal feels active, health-forward, and energetic. Login and home screen move to teal.

Why this matters

Right now: Login uses hardcoded blue, Workout uses teal/blueGrey custom colors, Home/Profile use Material 3 seed. They don't share a single color token — fixing bugs in one place doesn't fix the others. We need one source of truth.
